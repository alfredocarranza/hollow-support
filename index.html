Hollow support

Hollow is operated by Alfredo Carranza under the Carranza Technologies LLC brand.

Email alfredo@carranza.tech for Hollow app questions or a privacy request. Contact your Audiobookshelf server administrator for server accounts, library access, and server-side data.

Send a useful, safe report

Include your Hollow version and build, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, and Audiobookshelf server version if known. Describe the steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected, and what happened. If there is an error, include its exact text with private details removed.

Send only information needed for your request. Do not send passwords, access tokens, authentication headers, private server invitation links, payment details, or audiobook files. Do not send receipts or transaction identifiers with an initial request. Remove private server addresses, account names, library details, and personal content from any screenshot you choose to send.

Support correspondence

If you send email, you share your email address, message, and any attachments with the recipient and email providers. You can ask about access to or deletion of your support correspondence at alfredo@carranza.tech.
