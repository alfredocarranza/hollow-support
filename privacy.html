Privacy & your library

Hollow privacy policy

Hollow connects your device to Audiobookshelf servers you choose. It is operated by Alfredo Carranza under the Carranza Technologies LLC public brand. Hollow does not host your server or audiobook library, and you do not create a separate Hollow account.

The source reviewed for this version contains no identified developer telemetry upload path or advertising or analytics SDK. That is not a promise that no data is collected: your chosen server receives sign-in and listening requests, Apple processes App Store activity, and support email and developer-accessible reports are separate channels described below.

This policy distinguishes the app, your server operator, Apple, website hosting, and support. For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact alfredo@carranza.tech. Hollow uses the Carranza Technologies LLC public brand; Alfredo Carranza is the responsible operator.

Data exchanged with your chosen server

Hollow sends your username and password to the selected server when you sign in. The server returns an access token used to authenticate later requests. Requests may include library and book identifiers, search terms, playback session details, listening position and duration, completion status, and bookmark positions and titles.

The server supplies account information, library metadata, audio, chapters, and artwork. Those servers may log or process data according to policies set by their owners. Your server can see your network address and request information. Your server operator controls its accounts, storage, access rules, and retention practices. Contact that operator about its privacy policy and deletion options.

Hollow does not provide an audiobook catalog or create a separate Hollow account. Connecting to a server does not transfer its administration to Hollow support.

Data on your device

Hollow uses the iOS Keychain for saved sign-in information, including the access token. The sign-in flow sends the password to your server; the reviewed source does not explicitly save the password locally. Older versions stored the access token in app preferences. Hollow attempts to move that legacy token into Keychain, but a failed secure save can leave the older copy in preferences.

The server address, display username, and preferences are stored locally outside Keychain. Account-specific filenames use hashes, but that does not make retained account information anonymous or encrypt the contents of local files.

The app can retain downloaded audio, artwork, library metadata, listening progress, bookmarks, and records needed to recover or migrate older local data. System networking facilities may also retain caches or diagnostic information. Local storage is not a guarantee that every record is erased when a session ends.

If you use Settings to copy your server address, it is placed on the system clipboard. Playback information is also supplied to the system's Now Playing controls.

Connection security

Use a trusted HTTPS server address. HTTPS helps protect data in transit; your server operator manages its certificate and configuration. Hollow's handling of HTTP addresses and local-network connections depends on the app version and platform restrictions. Do not assume every connection is encrypted or send a password to an untrusted server.

Your server can supply media addresses, and redirects or streaming subresources may lead to other destinations. Requests are not guaranteed to stay at the original server address. Media links can contain temporary access information even when no password is visible. Treat them as sensitive and remove them from screenshots and support messages.

Retention, signing out, and deletion

Signing out ends the app's signed-in session and is intended to remove saved sign-in secrets and routing information. It does not erase all downloaded media, local listening history, bookmarks, migration records, or data held by your server.

Use the download and storage deletion controls available in your app version to remove local media you no longer want. Older or unrecognized files may not appear in the current account's list; contact support if storage remains or recovery is needed. Keep any needed copies before deleting local files or removing the app.

Removing the app can remove its app-container data, but device backups and Keychain items may persist. It is not a substitute for asking your server operator to delete its records. Progress or bookmark changes may be synchronized with your chosen server; deleting a local copy alone does not guarantee deletion on the server.

Support correspondence

Contact Support opens an email link; the app does not automatically attach diagnostics. If you send email, you share your email address, message, and any attachments with the recipient and email providers. Messages can contain personal data or details about your library. Please redact hostnames, usernames, IP addresses, passwords, access tokens, library details, and personal content before sending email. Send only what is necessary. The live support page asks for the Hollow version and build, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, Audiobookshelf server version if known, reproduction steps, and redacted error text. Do not send passwords, access tokens, authentication headers, private server invitation links, payment details, or audiobook files. Do not send receipts or transaction identifiers with an initial request. Remove private server addresses, account names, library details, and personal content from any screenshot you choose to send.

From publication of this policy, Alfredo Carranza will handle Hollow support manually in the existing inbox at alfredo@carranza.tech. We will use your email address and what you send to respond, troubleshoot and follow up, and keep requests in that inbox rather than forwarding or copying them into a new service. Your message is processed by the email providers involved in delivery.

We will keep routine support correspondence while working on your request and for follow-up, and remove it from active support storage within 90 calendar days after resolution, sooner when it is no longer needed. We will record an opaque local case reference, the actual resolution date and disposition, and review cleanup weekly without extending that deadline. Reopened requests remain open until resolved again. Correspondence needed for an ongoing dispute or a legal obligation may be kept longer for that purpose.

You may request deletion of a support conversation. Send access or deletion requests to alfredo@carranza.tech. We will confirm identity when necessary and explain applicable exceptions and provider-side retention. Removal from active support storage does not guarantee immediate or permanent deletion from provider Trash, backups or copies held by other recipients. The support deadline does not apply to Apple-hosted reports or separate report copies; our report-handling commitments are described below.

This website and GitHub Pages

These pages contain no JavaScript, analytics tags, advertising pixels, external fonts, or forms. The page code does not set cookies. Email links open your chosen email app.

Opening the Hollow support or privacy website in a browser sends a request to that website. The Hollow support website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub states that, when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's processing, cookies, retention, and privacy-request information. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for GitHub's records or that all hosting requests are free of cookies. This does not describe data sent to your Audiobookshelf server.

App Store purchases and access checks

Apple processes trial acquisitions, purchases, and purchase restoration through the App Store. Hollow uses Apple's StoreKit service to check access associated with Hollow and request its product catalog. These checks can run when you open the app, before you sign in to an Audiobookshelf server. Hollow processes verified information provided by Apple on your device, including product and transaction identifiers, an app-download identifier associated with your Apple Account and this app, purchase and original acquisition dates, and transaction status such as revocation. Catalog information includes product names and localized prices. These records support access checks. Trial and purchase availability depends on the offer presented by Apple and the checks described below.

Preparing a free trial or lifetime unlock checks the current offer and verified App Store information. Preparation does not start a trial or make a purchase. If the required checks pass, you can separately request the free 14-day trial, or review the current localized price and confirm a one-time lifetime purchase. The trial does not convert to a paid purchase. Hollow sends the selected request through Apple’s StoreKit service and uses verified transaction and app-download information on your device to match the response to the request, check the original trial date, expiry or revocation, determine access, and acknowledge a qualifying transaction. Hollow does not send your Audiobookshelf credentials or listening records as purchase options.

The app-download identifier is not your Apple Account email address or password, but it is a stable identifier for your Apple Account and this app. Hollow keeps App Store access checks separate from Audiobookshelf sign-in. The reviewed app does not send these purchase identifiers to your selected Audiobookshelf server or use them to label your listening records.

Apple's StoreKit and App Store services manage their own transaction records and may communicate with Apple. Apple processes App Store account, payment, purchase, and related information under its own terms. The StoreKit information used by Hollow does not provide the app with your payment card or bank-account details or your Apple Account sign-in credentials. See Apple's App Store & Privacy notice.

Restore Purchases, when available in your installed version, asks Apple to synchronize the app's transaction information and then checks verified records again. Apple may ask you to authenticate in a system prompt; Hollow does not receive the credentials entered there. Restore is not a new purchase, a guarantee of access, or a request to delete your server account or media.

The reviewed app keeps its access-check and catalog state in the running app's memory and does not write a separate purchase-history or entitlement database of its own. This is not a promise of immediate or secure erasure of memory or of StoreKit-managed data. Signing out of your Audiobookshelf server does not clear the running access-check state and does not delete your App Store purchase history. Deleting local downloads or removing Hollow is not a request to Apple to erase its records; Apple's notice explains its retention and privacy-request options.

App diagnostics and other data access

The source reviewed for this version contains no identified developer telemetry upload path or advertising or analytics SDK. Its commerce paths add no app-owned purchase logging, durable purchase store, or developer upload endpoint. These app-level findings do not exclude information made available to the operator through Apple. Production reports, TestFlight records and support email are separate channels.

Apple makes App Store reports available to developers, including aggregated usage information from users who opt in and purchase or refund receipt information. This availability does not mean Hollow's operator accesses or keeps every type of report. See Apple's App Store & Privacy notice.

If you test Hollow through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the app provider; this collection cannot be opted out of while testing. Feedback you choose to submit through TestFlight is also shared and may be associated with your name and email address. Hollow's release administration includes access to TestFlight tester identity, installation status, and installed app version. Apple's retention of TestFlight data is separate from the support-email retention policy above. See Apple's TestFlight & Privacy notice.

Operator-use commitments from publication: Alfredo Carranza will use Apple-accessible production reports only to administer Hollow's distribution, investigate reliability problems, and handle purchase, access or refund questions. He will use TestFlight tester, installation, build, crash, usage and feedback information only to administer testing and investigate or resolve issues raised by testing. Access and use will be limited to what is needed for those tasks.

For those purposes, we will work in Apple's services rather than create new downloads, exports or copies of report data in email, spreadsheets, issue trackers or other services. We will not sell report data, use it for advertising or audience profiling, combine it with Audiobookshelf accounts, library contents or listening records, or send it to other people or services, except where disclosure is required by law. These restrictions govern our handling from publication, including any report copies already held; they do not describe Apple's own processing.

We will stop using Apple-hosted records when they are no longer needed for these tasks. Apple controls their availability and retention in its services; stopping our use does not delete Apple's records. For any separately held report copies, we will review the continuing need weekly and remove routine copies from active operator storage at the review once they are no longer needed for these tasks. Copies needed for an ongoing dispute or a legal obligation may be kept longer for that purpose. This is a separate report-copy commitment, not an extension of the support-email rule or a promise that Apple, providers or earlier recipients have erased their copies.

You can ask Alfredo Carranza about access to or deletion of report information at the contact address in this policy. We will explain what we can identify and remove and any limits imposed by Apple's controls, provider retention or legal obligations.

Data can leave your device through requests to your selected server, interactions with Apple's StoreKit and App Store services, support messages you choose to send, and visits to this website. These activities have different recipients and retention practices, as described above.
